    Three things you
    should know.

    01 / MONDAY

    The Monday UPK Phenomenon

    Other schools start the week with a pop quiz. We start it with six straight periods of Vocational Training in one Workshop Lab. No room changes, no stair sprints, no essays, just tools, goggles and actual craftsmanship. It's the only day our backpacks are light and our hands are dirty, and we kind of love it.

    02 / TUE · THU · FRI

    The 7-Period Gauntlets

    Three days a week the timetable goes seven periods deep. Tuesday sends us across 6 rooms and 3 floors: Physics on floor 2, PE down on the ground floor, then straight back up to Math in 301. A ten-minute break isn't a break. It's timed parkour with a backpack.

    03 / FRIDAY · P7

    Friday Period 7: The Final Boss

    Seven periods, seven different rooms, and the boss fight waits at the very end: Physics in Room 210, while the rest of the world has already started the weekend. No save points, no respawns. Bring formulas, snacks and whatever is left of your will to live.
